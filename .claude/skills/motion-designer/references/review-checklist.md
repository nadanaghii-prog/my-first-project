# Motion review checklist (run before every render you deliver)

Look at 5–8 preview frames and one action as a contact sheet (every 2–3 frames). Answer each; fix
any "no" before rendering the MP4. Report the result briefly to the owner.

**Staging & story**
- [ ] One clear focal point per beat; the key moment can't be missed.
- [ ] Nothing important sits in the Reels UI zones (bottom ~320px, right ~140px) or under the title.
- [ ] Titles/text stay ≥ 2s and are readable at phone size.

**Timing & spacing**
- [ ] No living thing moves linearly; easing chosen per move (slow in / slow out).
- [ ] Speeds vary: something fast, something slow, holds of ≥ 0.3s on key poses.
- [ ] Characters each have their own rhythm (not all in sync).

**Physics feel**
- [ ] Every significant action has anticipation.
- [ ] Impacts squash; fast moves stretch; volume preserved; pivots at contact points.
- [ ] Loose parts (ears, tails, hair, clothes, leaves) follow through and settle — no dead stops.
- [ ] Moves travel on arcs; heads and limbs rotate around pivots.

**Life & appeal**
- [ ] Idle characters breathe/blink — nothing is frozen.
- [ ] Secondary actions support, never compete with, the main action.
- [ ] Key poses are exaggerated enough to read; the rest is calm.
- [ ] Shapes keep size and volume; light/shadow consistent; contact shadows present.
- [ ] Clear silhouettes; no "twins" (mirrored identical limbs/ears).

**Sound sync**
- [ ] Each sound effect lands on its frame (impact, pop, landing); music fades in/out.
